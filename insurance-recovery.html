Read this first

We are not public adjusters. We do not negotiate, adjust, or settle claims on your behalf, and we do not represent you to your insurance company.

What we do is establish the facts of the building. We provide documentation, estimating, planning, and owner representation — a written, defensible account of what a complete and code-compliant repair actually involves. If you need someone to negotiate the settlement itself, you want a licensed public adjuster, and we'll say so.

The problem

The estimate is written fast, by someone who wasn't there.

Adjusters carry heavy caseloads. Estimates get written from photographs, from a short walkthrough, or from a template. That's not necessarily bad faith — it's volume.

But the consequences land on you. Scope gets missed because it isn't visible in a photo. Code-required upgrades get omitted because nobody pulled the local requirements. Damage behind finished surfaces goes undocumented because nobody opened anything. Then repairs start, the real condition surfaces, and the money has already been allocated.

Missing scope

Work the building requires that never made it onto the estimate.

Unaccounted code

Current code requirements triggered by the repair, priced as if they weren't.

Undocumented damage

Conditions behind finishes that nobody opened up or photographed.

Wrong sequence

A repair plan that can't actually be built in the order it's written.
