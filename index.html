The calls we get

Most people call us
with one of these.

“Is this quote fair?”

We read estimates for a living. Bring three bids and we'll tell you which one is priced honestly and which one is missing half the scope.

“Is this contractor telling me the truth?”

Second opinions, in writing. If the work being recommended isn't necessary, you'll know before you sign anything.

“Can I do this myself?”

Sometimes yes. We'll tell you which parts you can handle, which parts need a licensed trade, and where doing it yourself gets expensive.

“Why did insurance deny that?”

A denied line item usually means missing documentation or an unaccounted code requirement — not a closed case.

If you've said one of these out loud, you don't need a contractor yet. You need an hour.

Tier 03  ·  Insurance & Property Recovery

Don't leave thousands of dollars on the table.

After a fire, a flood, a storm, or a vehicle through the front of the building, the first estimate you receive is a starting position — not a final answer.

Missing scope, unaccounted code requirements, and undocumented damage are normal. They are also worth real money. We review what you were sent, walk the property, read the photographs, and write down what's actually required to put the building back.

Scope of service

We provide documentation, estimating, planning, and owner representation. We are not public adjusters and we do not negotiate your claim with your carrier.

Process

How the first one goes.

Call or email

Tell us what you're facing in a couple of sentences. We'll say whether a consultation is the right starting point — and we'll say so if it isn't.

Phone, Zoom, or on site. We go through the documents, the estimates, the photographs, and the property, and answer everything you ask.

Fit

This isn't for everyone.

Saying so up front saves us both an afternoon.

  • People shopping for the lowest bid. If price is the only variable that matters, you don't need a consultant. You need three quotes and a coin.
  • Anyone looking to hire labor by the hour. We sell judgment and documentation. Tier 04 is the only tier where we build, and we take very few of those.
  • Small, well-understood projects. A consultation should save you more than it costs. If your project is simple and you already know the scope, we'll tell you to keep your $227.
  • Anyone who wants their claim negotiated. Negotiating a settlement with a carrier is public adjusting. It's a different license and a different job.
