Before you call

Four sentences is plenty.

None of this is required. It just gets us to something useful faster than starting from zero.

  • Which side it isA building, or a business
  • What you're facingRemodel, claim, dispute, systems, growth
  • Roughly the scaleA range is fine
  • Where you are in itThinking, bidding, or underway
  • Your timelineWeeks, months, urgent
If it's an insurance claim Have the carrier's estimate or scope sheet handy, plus the date of loss. Those two things answer most of the first round of questions.

What happens

After you call.

A short, free conversation

You explain the situation. We tell you which tier fits, what it costs, and whether it's worth doing at all. If the answer is that you don't need us, that's what you'll hear.

The consultation

Up to two hours by phone, Zoom, or on site — whichever the project calls for. $227. We review the documents, walk the property if needed, and answer everything you ask.

Your written summary

Findings, budget range, concerns, and a recommended sequence, in a document you keep. There's no obligation to do anything further, and no follow-up sales call.
