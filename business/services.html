On pricing

The consultation is a fixed price because it's a fixed thing: two hours and a written summary.

Tiers 02 through 04 are scoped after that conversation. Operational work varies too much to price honestly from a website — a company with eight employees and no scheduling system is a different job from a company with thirty and no chain of command. You'll get a number before any work starts, and it won't move without your agreement.

02

Build the system

When the business has outgrown the way you've been running it.

Growth exposes weak systems. Responsibilities blur. Scheduling gets harder. Employees start making decisions differently. The owner becomes the answer to every question.

That's usually not a people problem. It's a systems problem.

The goal isn't more paperwork. The goal is a business that doesn't require the owner to personally hold everything together.

Investment

Scoped after the consultation

Priced against what the operation actually needs, agreed before work starts.

What gets built

  • Roles and responsibilities
  • Chain of command
  • Scheduling systems
  • Pricing procedures
  • Customer communication
  • Employee and contractor workflows
  • Job documentation
  • Accountability systems
  • Standard operating procedures
  • Management structure

03

Fix the bottleneck

Something isn't working. Let's find out why.

We step into the operation, identify the bottleneck, and build a practical solution around the way your company actually works.

What this sounds like

  • Sales are growing but profit isn't.
  • Employees constantly need the owner's approval.
  • Scheduling is chaos.
  • Someone was promoted into management without defined authority.
  • Everyone is working hard and the business still feels harder to operate than it should.

Diagnose

Get inside the operation and find the actual constraint, which is rarely the one being complained about.

Design

Build the fix around how the company really runs, not around a template.

Implement

Put it in place and make sure it holds after we leave.

What this is not

Not theory. Not a seventy-page report that sits on a desk. A working solution.
