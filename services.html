Services & Pricing

What it costs, and what you get.

Published prices, because you shouldn't have to sit through a sales call to find out whether you can afford an answer. Every tier below produces written documentation. None of them require you to hire us to build anything.

Four tiers + two specialty lines

Why it's priced this way

You're not paying for hours. You're paying to reduce risk, prevent expensive mistakes, and make a better decision than you would have made alone.

A two-hour consultation that catches a $14,000 omission in a contractor's scope, or identifies code work the carrier didn't account for, has returned its cost many times before the invoice is even paid. That is the entire economic argument for hiring a consultant, and it's the honest one.
